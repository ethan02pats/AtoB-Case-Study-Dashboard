Action Plan — Re-engagement
Segment 1 — Activated, Never Transacted (24 people)
Day 1
Sort by fleet size3+ driver accounts are highest priority. Call those first — more drivers means more potential spend.
Day 2–3
Call them"Hey, noticed your card hasn't been used — is anything getting in the way? I can walk you through your first fill-up right now." Listen for: merchant gaps, card issues, or just forgot.
Day 3–5
Give them a reason to try it$0.05/gallon rebate on first $300 spend. Expires in 14 days. Create urgency to act.
Day 5–7
3-email sequenceHow to use the card → stations near their routes → savings comparison vs. competitors.
Day 14
Escalate or closeStill nothing? Offer a 5-min setup call. At Day 21 with zero engagement — flag to AE as a possible fit issue and move on.
Segment 2 — Churned Transactors (3 people)
Day 1
Diagnose firstDid spend drop gradually or stop dead? Sudden stop = card issue or competitor. Gradual = fleet change or business slowdown.
Day 2
Senior AE calls"We noticed a gap — did anything change with your fleet?" Don't lead with an offer. Just ask and actually listen.
Day 7
Win-back offer"Spend $500 in 30 days, get $25 fuel credit." Tie the incentive to actual behavior, not just goodwill.
Day 30
Decision pointStill nothing? Custom offer from leadership. Completely unresponsive? Mark as lost, document why — it's useful for future ICP work.
